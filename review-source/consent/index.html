INFORMED CHOICE

Care with clear boundaries.

For adults aged 18 and over

Every participant must freely agree. Each partner in a couples session gives their own consent. You may decline a topic, exercise or session. Ask how individual disclosures and joint records will be handled before couples work begins.

Scheduling is not treatment consent

A message or visit does not establish a doctor–patient relationship or consent to examination, treatment, recording, research or marketing. The treating professional must explain the service, alternatives, limitations, fees and privacy arrangements and obtain appropriate consent separately. No recording should occur without specific agreement.

Choose the appropriate support

Coaching is educational and does not diagnose, prescribe or replace mental-health treatment. Medical assessment and psychosexual counselling require an appropriately qualified provider acting within their scope. There are no guaranteed cures or outcomes. Online assessment may be insufficient and an in-person examination or referral may be needed.

Safety & emergencies

Seek urgent local medical care for severe or sudden symptoms or immediate danger. Do not wait for a WhatsApp reply. Where there is coercion, violence or lack of safety, individual specialist support may be more appropriate than joint coaching.
