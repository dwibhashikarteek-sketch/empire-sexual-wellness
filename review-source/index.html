QUESTIONS, WELCOMED

Before you begin.

Can I enquire from anywhere in India?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome across India. Online care is subject to the clinician’s assessment, availability and applicable professional rules. Some concerns require in-person care.

Is coaching the same as medical treatment?

No. Coaching focuses on education and communication. Medical symptoms need an appropriately qualified clinician; mental-health concerns may need a qualified mental-health professional.

Will my enquiry be private?

There are no patient forms here. WhatsApp shares your account and message with the recipient and processes data under its own terms. Avoid sending health records, images or intimate histories.
